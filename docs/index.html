The MS-CISBA Capstone

The capstone asks you to show, with a working prototype and a written argument, that you can integrate the four areas of the program: Data Analytics (BA), Data Mining and Management (DM), Systems Development (SS), and Networking and Cybersecurity (CN).

How to use this microsite

This microsite explains the capstone: what each deliverable is, why it is built the way it is, and how the parts fit together. WTClass holds the specific details about dates, submission, and scoring. Read a page here to understand a deliverable, then go to WTClass to turn it in.

The resources below come in three kinds:

  1. Figures show the ideas the capstone rests on. Figure 01 draws the four areas as one space. Figure 02 draws one example project three ways. Figure 03 shows how to draw your own project for the proposal. Each figure steps or switches with the buttons under it.
  2. Guides explain the deliverables. Your repository shows where every deliverable lives. The other guides cover the portfolio and bio, the proposal, the synthesis prototype, the paper, the presentation, and the debrief. Four further pages describe the areas and the courses behind them.
  3. Protocol pages give the written procedures that replace meetings: who acts at each step, which file each step leaves in your repository, and in what order.

A suggested order: read Figure 01 first, then the repository guide, then the guide for whichever deliverable is due next. Before you write the proposal, read Figure 03 and the proposal cycle.
