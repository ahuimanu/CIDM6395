The MS-CISBA Capstone

In the capstone you build a working prototype and write an argument for it, and together they have to show that you can integrate the four areas of the program: Data Analytics (BA), Data Mining and Management (DM), Systems Development (SS), and Networking and Cybersecurity (CN).

How to use this microsite

This site explains what each deliverable is and why it is built the way it is. Specific details about dates, submission, and scoring are in WTClass. You submit every deliverable the same way, by committing it to your private repository in the course organization.

If you are new to the site, read Figure 01 first; it draws the four areas as one space, and the rest of the site refers back to it. Then read Your repository, which gives the path of every file you will commit. After that, read the guide for whatever is due next. Before you start the proposal, read Figure 03 and the proposal cycle.

Figures 01 and 03 step forward with the Next button, and Figure 02 switches between its three views. Each guide covers one deliverable, and four pages cover the areas and the courses behind them. The protocol pages set out the written procedures we use in place of meetings.
