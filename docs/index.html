The MS-CISBA Capstone

The capstone asks you to show, with a working prototype and a written argument, that you can integrate the four areas of the program: Data Analytics (BA), Data Mining and Management (DM), Systems Development (SS), and Networking and Cybersecurity (CN). This site explains the ideas behind the capstone. Dates, submission, and scoring are on the course pages in WTClass, and those pages link here.
