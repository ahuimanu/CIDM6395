Protocol · The proposal cycle

The proposal cycle

We settle the design of your project in writing rather than in a meeting. You commit a proposal, I commit a decision and numbered questions, you commit your answers, and I settle the decision.

The steps

Every file in this table stays in your repository. You write the files in proposal/; I write only feedback/proposal.md.

StepWho actsWhat they doFile left in your repositoryWhen

The decision

I make one of four decisions. A greenlight means the proposal is specific and feasible and meets the requirements, so you build. A conditional greenlight approves the direction but asks you to answer the numbered questions before major build work; it becomes a greenlight once your answers settle the conditions. Revise before greenlight means the idea has promise but something required is not yet clear enough to approve, so you revise proposal/proposal.md and answer the questions, and I review the revision. Not evaluable means the proposal is missing, inaccessible, or too incomplete to judge.

Your response

Answer each question under its own number, in the order I asked them, and give the specific thing the question asks for: a file path, a source, a record count, a decision. If an answer changes the proposal, say so and update proposal/proposal.md to match.

Some answers are easier to show than to write, like a data source open in a browser or a first script running. For those you may record a video of up to five minutes and link it from the answer it supports.

Why in writing

A conversation leaves nothing either of us can check later. Here the proposal, my questions, and your answers become the record of what we agreed, and at the final review I compare the delivered work against it. Tool approvals go into the same record: I approve or decline each non-Python tool in feedback/proposal.md.

Specific details about dates, submission, and scoring are in WTClass. The proposal instructions are on the WTClass assignment page; what the proposal must show is in the proposal guide.
