Evolution feedback

Each appraisal you commit gets a written response from me, committed to your repository, with a question to take into the next one.

The steps

Your appraisal and my feedback file both stay in your repository. The score goes only to WTClass.

StepWho actsWhat they doFile left in your repositoryWhen
1StudentCommit the appraisal as the README of its evolution folder in your private course repository.evolution-N/README.mdBy the due date posted in WTClass
2ProfessorRead the appraisal and the artifacts it cites, and commit feedback written to you.feedback/evolution-N.mdAfter the due date
3ProfessorEnter the score in WTClass.None (the score is in WTClass)With the feedback
4StudentRead the feedback and carry its question into the next appraisal.The next evolution-N/README.mdBefore the next due date

The feedback file

feedback/evolution-N.md says what is working and the evidence that shows it, what needs attention as specific items you can check, and how ready your integration thinking is for the capstone. It ends with one question. The score is in WTClass, not in the file; the file is about the work.

Using it

Answer the closing question in your next appraisal, not in a reply to me. When an appraisal takes up the question, it shows your thinking developing from one evolution to the next, which is what the rubric's integration dimension rewards. By the time you write the proposal you will have four of these files, a running record of how your view of the four areas changed.

The feedback/ folder holds only my files. Leave them where they are and do not edit them.

Specific details about dates, submission, and scoring are in WTClass. What an appraisal must argue is in the portfolio guide.
