Figure 03 · Drawing your project

The Minimum Demo

The proposal includes your own drawing of the project. The drawing has four parts, added in order: the pretext, the process flow, a solid box around the first slice, and dashed boxes around what the full project adds. This figure builds one drawing step by step, then gives the blank form.

The blank form

Draw your own project in this form. Replace each box with an area code (BA, DM, SS, or CN) in the order the areas act, and add or remove boxes to fit your flow. Commit the drawing as proposal/diagram.png or proposal/diagram.svg. Any drawing tool is acceptable, and so is a clear photograph of a hand drawing.

Reading the figure

The solid box is a promise. At the end of the term a reviewer starts the prototype from its README and checks that everything inside the box runs on real data. The box must contain SS, because the prototype must function, and at least two of the other three areas. It must also contain one complete path, from the source data to an output a user sees. A slice that builds every box halfway has no such path.

The dashed boxes are a forecast. They do not run this term. The synthesis paper and the presentation explain them, and they show that the first slice is a sample of a larger project, not a separate small one.
