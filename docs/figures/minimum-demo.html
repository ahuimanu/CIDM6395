The Minimum Demo

Your proposal includes your own drawing of the project. It has four parts, added in this order: the pretext, the process flow, a solid box around the synthesis prototype, and dashed boxes around what the full project adds. Below, one drawing is built step by step, followed by the two models from orientation and a blank form.

The two models from orientation

Orientation showed two models in this form, redrawn here and updated for Fall 2026. In the orientation slides neither solid box included SS. Because the prototype has to run, the Fall 2026 rule puts SS in every minimum demo, so in both models below the solid box now extends to include it. The slides also label CN as SN. Your own drawing will look different from either.

Primary model

One chain: CN supplies or secures the data, DM stores it, BA analyzes it, and SS delivers the result. The minimum demo runs the whole chain at a small scale.

Alternative model

BA, DM, and SS form the chain, and CN surrounds it as the network, access rules, and controls the chain runs inside. The minimum demo covers all three areas in the chain.

The blank form

Draw your own project in this form. Replace each box with an area code (BA, DM, SS, or CN) in the order the areas act, and add or remove boxes to fit your flow. Commit the drawing as proposal/diagram.png or proposal/diagram.svg. Any drawing tool is acceptable, and so is a clear photograph of a hand drawing.

Reading the figure

The solid box is what you promise to deliver. At the end of the term I start the prototype from its README and check that everything inside the box runs on real data. The box has to hold SS and at least two other areas, and one complete path from the source data to an output a user sees; a prototype with every box half built has no such path.

The dashed boxes do not run this term. The paper and the presentation explain them, and they make the case that the prototype is a sample of a larger project. Figure 02 shows the example from three angles, and Figure 01 shows the space the project sits in.
