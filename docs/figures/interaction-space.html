Figure 01 · Where the capstone lives

The Interaction Space

Four curricular areas drawn as overlapping regions. The overlaps are where the capstone lives: a practice where two areas meet, a minimum demo where three meet, and at the center, where all four meet, the account you give of the whole: the synthesis paper, the presentation, and the debrief.

Reading the figure

The picture is explanatory, not prescriptive. It does not tell you what to build. It tells you where any project you might build is located, and therefore what you must be able to point to when you claim synthesis: the practice in each overlap your project occupies, and the artifact in your repository that proves it. Every piece of the final package has a place in it. The four area appraisals in your portfolio are the four regions. The prototype is a point in a triple overlap or the center. The paper, the presentation, and the debrief are written, shown, and defended from the center.
