Figure 01 · Where the capstone lives

The Interaction Space

The four areas of the program drawn as overlapping regions. Where two areas overlap, there is a recognizable kind of work. Where three overlap, a synthesis prototype can sit. The center, where all four meet, is the full project, and it is where the paper, the presentation, and the debrief speak from.

Area names follow the 2026–2027 Graduate Catalog. Elsewhere on this site and in course documents they are BA, DM, SS, and CN.

Reading the figure

The figure does not tell you what to build. It tells you where a project sits once you have chosen one, and so what you will need to point to when you claim synthesis: the kind of work in each overlap your project occupies, and the file in your repository that shows it. Every part of the final package has a place here. Your four appraisals describe the four regions, the prototype sits in a three-way overlap or at the center, and the paper, presentation, and debrief account for the whole.

Figure 02 draws one project inside this space three ways, as a chain, a stack, and a hub. Figure 03 shows how to draw your own.
