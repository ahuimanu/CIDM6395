Data Analytics (BA)

Data Analytics is the area that answers the question in your pretext. It takes data that DM has prepared and turns it into a finding that a person can act on.

From prepared data to a finding

The findings BA produces take many forms, among them a rate, a forecast, a classification, a comparison, or a chart that makes a pattern visible. In the example project used across this site, BA computes complaints per thousand loans for each lender and flags the lenders whose rates stand out, and the examiner decides which lenders to review on the strength of that flag. Without BA, the system would store and move the data without telling the examiner anything about it.

BA depends on the other areas at both ends of its work. Its input has to arrive in a known shape, which is the work of DM; its output reaches a user only through software, which is the work of SS; and when the data or the result is confidential, CN determines who may see it. In Figure 01 these three overlaps are the data pipeline (with DM), decision delivery (with SS), and security analytics (with CN).

What to show in your repository

In the proposal, say what the user would lose if BA were removed; in the example project, the examiner would receive the data but no flag. In the repository, BA appears as code that computes the result, either a script or a notebook that the pipeline calls. A chart pasted into the paper does not show that the computation exists.
