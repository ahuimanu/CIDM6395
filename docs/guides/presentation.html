Guide · The presentation

The presentation

The presentation is a video of five to fifteen minutes, with slides, that connects the other parts of your capstone. It shows the prototype working and relates it to the competence your portfolio and bio document and to the projection your paper makes.

Connecting the pieces

Each of the other deliverables answers one question. The portfolio and bio show what you can do, the prototype shows the areas working together, and the paper argues for the synthesis and projects it a year forward. The presentation is the one deliverable in which a viewer meets all of them at once, so its job is to show how they connect: which work in your portfolio prepared you for which part of the prototype, and how the prototype supports the projection in the paper. A video that shows only the prototype leaves those connections for the viewer to make.

Showing the prototype

Spend most of the time on the prototype running on real data, along its complete path from the data arriving to the output a user sees, since a running prototype is the evidence that the design works. As each step runs, say which area is acting; in the example used on this site, the load is SS and the name matching is DM. The chain view in Figure 02 follows the same order a demonstration does.

The forecast

Close with the paper's projection: what the full project adds over a year, and why it is feasible. Use the dashed boxes from your proposal drawing (Figure 03). The forecast is persuasive to the extent that the prototype has already done, at a smaller scale, each kind of work the full project needs.

An order that works

Open with about a minute on who you are and the portfolio work the project builds on, followed by a minute on the pretext: who has the problem and what decision they make. Take a minute or two on the design, using your proposal drawing, give most of the remaining time to the demonstration, and finish with a minute or two on the forecast.

Length and format

The video runs at least five and at most fifteen minutes. The slides and the video go in deliverables/, as listed below, and a video link must open without a login so that I can watch it without your account.
