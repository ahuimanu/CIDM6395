Guide · The presentation

The presentation

The presentation is a 5 to 15 minute video with slides, and it is the one place a viewer sees all your work together: the portfolio and bio, the prototype, and the paper.

Connecting the pieces

Each of the other deliverables stands on its own. The portfolio and bio show what you can do, the prototype shows the areas working together, and the paper argues for the synthesis and carries it a year forward. A viewer of the presentation should come away seeing how they connect: this background, documented in this portfolio, made this prototype possible, and the paper projects it this far. If the video shows only the prototype, the viewer has to make those connections without you.

Showing the prototype

Spend most of the time on the prototype running on real data, along its complete path from the data arriving to the output a user sees. Describing a project is not the same as showing that it works. As each step runs, say which area is acting: the load is SS, the name matching is DM, and so on. The chain view in Figure 02 follows the same order a demonstration does.

The forecast

Close with the paper's projection: what the full project adds over a year and why it is feasible. Use the dashed boxes from your proposal drawing (Figure 03). The forecast is convincing to the degree that the prototype has already done each kind of work the full project needs, at a smaller scale.

A workable order

Open with about a minute on who you are and the portfolio work the project builds on, then a minute on the pretext: who has the problem and what they decide. Take a minute or two on the design, using your proposal drawing. Give most of the remaining time to the demonstration, and finish with a minute or two on the forecast.

Length and format

The video runs at least five and at most fifteen minutes. Five minutes is rarely enough to show a complete path, and a video that runs past fifteen is usually listing parts rather than showing how they fit. The slides and video go in deliverables/, as listed below; a video link must open without a login.
